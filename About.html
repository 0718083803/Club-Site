Who are we

We are a group of high schoolers who enjoy and are fascinated by building and creation of new things. We are all about learning and exploring new technologies and developing new softwares. As long as you are 13-18 you can join us and have fun building and meeting new people with the same interests

Our Mission

Our mission is to spread digital literacy. Teach people about Artificial Intelligence,essence of technology and how to use it. We want to spread and teach the ethical use of technology. Inspire teenagers to get into the tech field. Help guide careers in the field of technology Build the new generation of computers scientists,software developers and AI enginners.

Our Motto

Think. Build. Break. Repeat. Succeed.

We believe in the philosophy of thinking of an idea, quickly actin on it to get a prototype and working model, try to break it to test and fullly understand how it works and repeat that process until eventually you make a breakthrough and succeed

Our Values

Our core valies are Respect, Tolerance, Teamwork, Curiosity, Creativity, Tolerance, Collaboration , Persistance

Our Activities

We are involved in a lot of activities like Coding, projects, workshops, and technology exploration and many more. What we just need is you time and dedication with passion to do everything
